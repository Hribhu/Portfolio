01 / About

Off the clock — Odisha coastline

A telecom engineer who kept following the signal — until it turned into data.

I'm currently completing my B.Tech in Electronics & Telecommunication at KIIT, Bhubaneswar, where the coursework on communication systems and networking gave me a genuine feel for how information moves — through fibre, over LTE, across a switching stack. That grounding turned out to be the best possible entry point into analytics: both disciplines are, at their core, about extracting a clean signal out of noisy data.

Over the last two years I've split my time between telecom infrastructure work — studying transmission techniques, optical fibre, and 2G/3G/4G/LTE architecture at BSNL — and building full-stack analytics tools, most recently a supply-chain intelligence dashboard for Bharti Airtel that turned scattered spreadsheets into a live operational health score. I like problems that sit at that intersection: systems with a lot of moving parts, where the job is to make the important part visible.

03 / Projects

Things I've built end to end

Customer Purchase Behavior Analysis

Python · MySQL · Power BI

An end-to-end shopping-behaviour analysis pipeline built across 3,900 transactions and 18 attributes. Cleaned the raw data and engineered features with pandas — imputing 37 missing values, segmenting customers, and running more than 10 targeted business queries against a MySQL backend.

Closed the loop with an interactive Power BI dashboard surfacing revenue trends, subscription behaviour and core KPIs, built to support real business recommendations rather than just describe the data.

Atmospheric Monitoring System

Raspberry Pi · ML · Python · ThingSpeak

A real-time atmospheric monitoring rig on a Raspberry Pi 4B, integrating DHT11, MQ135 and LDR sensors with an I2C LCD readout for on-device display. On top of the sensor stack, I trained a logistic regression classifier over temperature, humidity, light and gas-concentration features, reaching 89.5% classification accuracy.

Streamed five environmental parameters to ThingSpeak IoT every 15 seconds for live cloud visualization, historical trend analysis and remote monitoring — a small, complete hardware-to-cloud loop.

Banking Customer Insights & Risk Segmentation

Python · MySQL · Power BI

An end-to-end banking analytics pipeline analysing 3,000 customer records across 25 attributes spanning income, loans, deposits and credit exposure. Handled data cleaning, income-band segmentation and correlation analysis with pandas and Seaborn on data pulled via MySQL, surfacing patterns across nationality, occupation and risk weighting.

Delivered as an interactive Power BI dashboard visualising customer demographics, loyalty classification and financial KPIs — built to support risk assessment and business decision-making.
